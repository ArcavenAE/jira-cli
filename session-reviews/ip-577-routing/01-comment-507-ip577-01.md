### New sub-class: resolved-by-shipping doc-label family

This cycle added a specific, high-cost sub-class of the peer-artifact sweep gap: **forward-reference placeholder labels** inserted at spec-design time that survive the entire bundle because each per-story TDD loop only touches its own story's scope. The population only becomes visible at wave-integration or bundle-close boundaries.

**Observed instances in SOH-COMMENT-CRUD-1 (6-story, 11-BC bundle):**

- **Wave C — 4 sweeps across 3 artifact layers (docs/CLAUDE.md/src):**
  - PR #618: `docs/specs/comment-crud.md` and `docs/specs/adf-recursion-depth.md` carried stale stub labels.
  - PR #619: `src/` comment strings carried stale stub labels.
  - Wave-C integration adversary passes 1 → 2 → 3: additional variants caught after each sweep. Four passes required to fully clear the population.
- **Wave D — BC Source sync (1 sweep):**
  - 9 rows in `bc-3-issue-write.md` (BC-3.5.002..010) carried `"(relocates at F4)"` and `"citations updated at delivery"` placeholders instead of actual shipped symbols (`handle_comment_delete`, `handle_comment_edit`, `handle_comment_view`, `delete_comment`, `update_comment`, `get_comment`, `tests/comment_delete.rs`, `tests/comment_edit.rs`, `tests/comment_view.rs`).
  - Required a separate factory-artifacts sync commit.

**Total cost:** 6 fix sweeps, 3 separate PRs/commits, across 3 artifact layers.

**Root cause** (same as #507 but at the doc-label level): spec artifacts are written during design phase with forward-references to symbols that don't exist yet. Per-story TDD loops are scoped to their story's own files; no loop has visibility into the full cross-story population.

---

### Proposed remedy (extends current #507 scope)

Add a **mandatory final-wave delivery step** to the wave-close / bundle-close checklist, distinct from the per-fix-round sibling sweep:

```
Mandatory final-wave repo-wide label grep (run ONCE at the last story of each bundle):
1. grep -r '"citations updated at delivery"\|"relocates at\|TODO\|FIXME\|stub' .factory/specs/prd/
2. grep -r '// TODO\|// FIXME\|// stub\|// deferred to' src/
3. grep -r 'deferred to <story-id>' docs/specs/
```

The grep catches the **full population** at once rather than discovering variants one family at a time through adversary passes. This is complementary to the per-fix-round sibling sweep in #507: the sibling sweep catches same-fix propagation misses; the final-wave grep catches design-time forward-references that survive the entire delivery pipeline.

**Severity:** LOW process-gap. In a 6-story bundle it cost 6 fix sweeps and 3 PRs; in larger bundles it will compound proportionally.
