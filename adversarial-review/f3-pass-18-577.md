**Story:** S-577-1
**Severity:** MEDIUM

**Fix applied (round 18):**

- Task 3b inserted: ordered migration task for the 6+2 sites with do-not-commit guard (hold at compile-failure until all match arms updated)
- Preamble distinguishes the compile-gate phase from the subsequent red-test phase
