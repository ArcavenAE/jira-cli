What this proves:
  src/api/auth.rs's DEFAULT_OAUTH_SCOPES `concat!` literal previously held 8
  classic/CMDB scopes. This story expands it to the finalized 16-scope
  full-parity set: the 8 pre-existing scopes UNCHANGED IN POSITION, followed
  by `manage:jira-project` (closes the component-write OAuth gap —
  `jr component create/edit/delete/rename`), followed by the 7 granular
  Jira-Software/Agile scopes required by `jr board`/`jr sprint`
  (`read:board-scope:jira-software`, `read:board-scope.admin:jira-software`,
  `read:sprint:jira-software`, `write:board-scope:jira-software`,
  `read:project:jira`, `read:issue-details:jira`, `read:jql:jira`).

  This single test function carries all four ACs:
    - AC-001: a per-scope assert! loop over all 16 required scopes, PLUS a
      total-count canary (exactly 16, split_whitespace().count()), PLUS a
      RAW exact-literal assert_eq! against the full expected string
      (ordering AND spacing — deliberately not normalized, so a stray
      leading/trailing/double space would fail this even though it wouldn't
      fail the per-scope loop) — this is the error-path proof: any drift
      from the pinned literal (missing scope, wrong order, stray whitespace)
      fails loudly here before it ever reaches the OAuth authorize URL as a
      malformed `%20%20` scope parameter.
    - AC-002: the same test, updated in the SAME commit as the AC-001
      implementation edit, per BC-1.3.023's pinning-test lockstep
      obligation — this transcript IS that "run green" proof.
    - AC-003: `manage:jira-project` is asserted present via both the
      per-scope loop and the exact-literal comparison, distinct from and
      in addition to `write:jira-work` (the component-write gap closure).
    - AC-004: two negative assertions — `!scopes.contains("view:team:teams")`
      and `!scopes.contains("view:membership:teams")` — prove Workstream D
      (Teams scopes) is NOT present; ADR-0026 Decision 2/4 defers Teams to a
      future S6 spike. (The RED-GATE mutation check that these negative
      assertions actually fire on a re-introduced Teams scope was performed
      manually during implementation per the story's own test note, not
      re-run for this capture — re-running it would require a source edit,
      which the demo-recorder role does not perform.)
